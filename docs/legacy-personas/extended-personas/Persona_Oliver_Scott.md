## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: What are the common triggers for Attacks of Opportunity in PF1e, and how does casting provoke?
- Expected Answer (English): Leaving a threatened square without a 5‑foot step, making a ranged attack in melee, standing up, and casting a spell (unless defensively) provoke. Casting defensively is a Concentration check to avoid provoking.

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: How do 5‑foot steps interact with full‑attacks in PF1e?
- Expected Answer (English): If you don’t otherwise move in a round, you can take a 5‑foot step before, during, or after a full‑attack to adjust positioning without provoking.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: How is Concentration DC calculated for casting defensively or when taking damage?
- Expected Answer (English): Concentration DC varies by situation; casting defensively is DC 15 + double the spell level; taking damage is 10 + damage taken + spell level; other cases (grapple, vigorous motion) have specific DC formulae.
