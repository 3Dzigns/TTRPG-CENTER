## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: When should I use Recall Knowledge in PF2e and what DCs should I set as a GM?
- Expected Answer (English): Use Recall Knowledge any time lore could help: weaknesses, immunities, society facts, planar traits. Set DC by level of the creature/subject and adjust for rarity. Success gives useful but not exhaustive info; crit success offers precise weaknesses/resistances.

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: How does Proficiency by Level affect skill checks compared to 5e?
- Expected Answer (English): PF2e adds level to trained characters, so checks scale tightly with encounter level. Degrees of success and DC-by-level tables keep difficulty consistent. Low-level PCs can still try high DCs but need strong bonuses and aid.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: What are good early control options for a Witch or Occult caster?
- Expected Answer (English): Fear, Hideous Laughter, and Color Spray target Will; Tanglefoot and Grease hinder actions/positioning. Evil Eye hex and Protective Ward support allies. Focus management is key; Refocus and hex cantrips maintain presence.
