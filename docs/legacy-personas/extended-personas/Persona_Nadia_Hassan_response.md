**Question:** What’s the difference between a swift action and an immediate action in PF1e?
**Expected:** A swift action is on your turn and costs your swift for that round. An immediate action is taken out of turn (as a reaction) and uses up your swift action for your next turn.
**Answer:** In PF1e, a swift action can only be performed on your turn and counts as your swift action for that turn, while an immediate action can be performed at any time, even when it is not your turn. If you use an immediate action before your turn, it counts as your swift action for the upcoming turn, and you cannot use another immediate or swift action until after your next turn. Additionally, you cannot use an immediate action if you are flat-footed [1].
