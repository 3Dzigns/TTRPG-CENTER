**Expected:** Start with a CMB vs CMD after any needed touch. On later rounds, a standard action to maintain (CMB vs CMD). Success lets you damage, move, pin, or tie up; failure ends the grapple.
**Answer:** The step-by-step process for Grapple in Pathfinder 1st Edition (PF1e) is as follows:

1. **Initiate Grapple**: Use a standard action to make a grapple combat maneuver. Make an attack roll and add your Combat Maneuver Bonus (CMB) to the roll. The target must roll against your Combat Maneuver Defense (CMD).

2. **Success**: If your grapple attempt is successful, you and the target are now grappling. The target is considered grappled, and you can choose to deal damage or maintain the grapple.

3. **Maintain Grapple**: On your turn, you can choose to maintain the grapple as a standard action. You make another grapple check (CMB vs. CMD). If successful, you continue to grapple the target.

4. **Options on Successful Maintain**: When you maintain a grapple, you can choose to:
   - Deal damage to the target (1d4 for a Medium creature).
   - Move the grappled target (you can move with the target, but your movement is halved).
   - Perform a combat maneuver against the grappled target (such as pinning or throwing).

These steps allow you to effectively grapple and control your opponent during combat [2].
