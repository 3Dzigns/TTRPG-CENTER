## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: How should I implement Dragonmarks in PF2e for an Eberron campaign?
- Expected Answer (English): Treat Dragonmarks as ancestry feats (or a dedication archetype) that grant themed focus spells or skill feats and scale at higher levels. Use lesser/greater mark feats as milestones. Dragonshards can be consumables that heighten mark spells or reduce focus costs.

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: What is a balanced PF2e approach to Warforged ancestry?
- Expected Answer (English): Start from Construct traits without immunities that break healing; give Repair and Treat Wounds compatibility. Offer heritages like Juggernaut (sturdy), Skirmisher (agile), and Envoy (integrated tools). Resistances should be small (e.g., res 2 physical at low level) and scale by feats.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: How do I model elemental binding tech (lightning rail/airships) with PF2e rules?
- Expected Answer (English): Use Vehicles plus simple subsystem checks (Arcana/Crafting) for upkeep and hazards. Elemental rings act like runes that boost speed/handling. Dragonshard fuel provides limited-use boosts; failures create electrical or planar hazards.
