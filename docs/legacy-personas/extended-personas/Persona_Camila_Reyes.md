## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: How does PF2e’s three-action economy work in combat (including reactions and Multiple Attack Penalty)?
- Expected Answer (English): Each turn you have three actions and one reaction. Most activities cost 1–3 actions (Stride, Strike, Step, Raise a Shield, 2-action spells, 3-action heal, etc.). Reactions (like Attack of Opportunity) happen off-turn when a trigger occurs. Making more than one Strike in a turn applies Multiple Attack Penalty to later Strikes (–5 then –10; –4/–8 with Agile). Free actions require a trigger and do not consume actions.

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: As a battlefield control-focused Wizard, what strong early-level options should I consider?
- Expected Answer (English): Prioritize spells that change the battlefield or debuff foes: Grease, Color Spray, Fear, Tanglefoot, Hydraulic Push, and Illusory Object for soft cover/line-of-sight tricks. Use cantrips like Electric Arc for efficient damage when control isn’t ideal. Take skill feats that support initiative/perception and consider archetypes (e.g., Cleric, Druid) that add flexible control tools.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: What’s the difference between Exploration, Encounter, and Downtime modes, and how do they affect initiative?
- Expected Answer (English): Exploration mode covers travel/investigation between encounters—your chosen activity (Avoid Notice, Defend, Scout, etc.) can impact starting conditions. Encounter mode is structured rounds using the three-action economy and initiative (often Perception, but other skills can set it based on what you were doing). Downtime covers long tasks (crafting, retraining, earning income) with checks over hours/days.

- Question: ¿Cómo funciona la economía de tres acciones de PF2e en combate (incluyendo reacciones y la penalización por múltiples ataques)?
