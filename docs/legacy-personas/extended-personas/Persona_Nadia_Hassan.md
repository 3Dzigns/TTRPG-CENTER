## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: In PF1e, how do CMB and CMD work for grapple, and what actions are required to start and maintain a grapple?
- Expected Answer (English): CMB is attacker’s BAB + Str mod + size mods; CMD is 10 + BAB + Str + Dex + size + misc. To start a grapple, make a melee touch (if required) then a CMB check vs CMD; maintaining the grapple is a standard action each round with another CMB check, and success lets you choose options (damage, pin, move, etc.).

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: What’s the difference between a swift action and an immediate action in PF1e?
- Expected Answer (English): A swift action is on your turn and costs your swift for that round. An immediate action is taken out of turn (as a reaction) and uses up your swift action for your next turn.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: How do iterative attacks work with full‑attack in PF1e?
- Expected Answer (English): With BAB +6/+11/+16 you gain extra attacks at –5 per step when using a full‑attack; all apply pending modifiers. You can insert a 5‑foot step during the full‑attack if you haven’t otherwise moved.
