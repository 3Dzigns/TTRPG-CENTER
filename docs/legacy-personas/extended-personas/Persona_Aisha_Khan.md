## Q&A (English)

### Question 1 (English)
- Question: In PF1e, what’s the step‑by‑step for Grapple from start to maintain, and what options can you apply on a successful maintain?
- Expected Answer (English): Start with a CMB vs CMD after any needed touch. On later rounds, a standard action to maintain (CMB vs CMD). Success lets you damage, move, pin, or tie up; failure ends the grapple.

### Question 2 (English)
- Question: How do Trip and Prone interact in PF1e, and do you provoke when tripping?
- Expected Answer (English): A Trip is a CMB vs CMD; if you succeed, target becomes prone (–4 melee attacks, –4 AC vs melee, can stand as move provoking). Tripping might provoke unless you have Improved Trip or a weapon with Trip.

### Question 3 (English)
- Question: How is CMD calculated, and what common modifiers apply to it?
- Expected Answer (English): CMD is 10 + BAB + Str + Dex + size + misc. Conditions like prone, flat‑footed, or being entangled can affect CMD, as can feats and deflection/insight bonuses.
