## Q2 (English)
**Question:** How do 5‑foot steps interact with full‑attacks in PF1e?
**Expected:** If you don’t otherwise move in a round, you can take a 5‑foot step before, during, or after a full‑attack to adjust positioning without provoking.
**Answer:** In Pathfinder 1st Edition, taking a 5-foot step does not count as movement and does not provoke an attack of opportunity. You can take a 5-foot step before, during, or after your other actions in the round, including a full attack. However, you cannot take a 5-foot step in the same round that you move any distance. Therefore, if you are making a full attack, you can still take a 5-foot step as part of that action, provided you haven't moved any other distance that round [2].
