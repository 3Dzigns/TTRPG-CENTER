## Persona 1: Aaliyah Vance

* **Age:** 19
* **Gender:** Female
* **Background:** American college student, new to TTRPGs, curious about magitech.
* **Religion:** Non-religious

**Questions & Expected Outcomes**

1. *“How do Warforged Juggernaut prestige class abilities from Eberron 3.5 translate into Pathfinder 1E rules?”*

   * **Expected Outcome:** A Warforged Juggernaut becomes a prestige archetype for Warforged Fighters/Barbarians, granting natural armor, fortification, and construct immunities step by step.
2. *“Is the Pathfinder Construct type compatible with Eberron’s Warforged racial healing rules?”*

   * **Expected Outcome:** Pathfinder Construct rules conflict, but using Warforged subtype with hybrid healing (half from cure spells, full from repair spells) matches Eberron intent.
3. *“Can you still apply enhancement bonuses from Pathfinder magic weapons to an integrated Warforged weapon?”*

   * **Expected Outcome:** Yes, integrated Warforged weapons follow normal enhancement rules, but GM must decide if they count as “wielded” for feats.
